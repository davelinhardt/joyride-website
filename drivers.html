Frequently asked

Operator questions, answered.

Who is Joyride for?

Licensed car service, chauffeur, and executive transportation companies — from an owner-operator with one commercial sedan to a fleet of thirty. If you carry passengers commercially and professionally, you're who we're built for.

How does Joyride bring me more work?

Riders come to Joyride for the trips they can't risk getting wrong — early flights, events, evenings out, family trips. Those bookings are made in advance and assigned to operators in the market, which means predictable, plannable work rather than opportunistic street hails.

Do I set my own rates?

Yes. You publish rates for each class of service you offer. The rider sees the price before booking and it doesn't change afterward, so there's no negotiating and no discount pressure.

Do the customers belong to me?

Your clients, ratings, and reviews stay with your company. Riders who liked their trip can request you again, and you can set up recurring trips with regulars directly.

Can I run Joyride alongside my existing accounts?

Yes. Most operators use Joyride to fill open capacity around their corporate accounts and direct bookings. You control which vehicles and hours you make available.

What do you expect from my chauffeurs?

The core promise to riders is that we never cancel. Once a trip is assigned, it runs — on time, in the vehicle class booked, with a professional at the wheel. Operators who hold that standard get more bookings.
