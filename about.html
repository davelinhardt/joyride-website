About

We built the ride we wanted to book.

Joyride is a premium transportation marketplace. We connect riders with licensed car service operators and professional drivers — and we hold every trip to the same standard, so booking a car stops being a gamble.

Why we exist

Getting a car became a coin flip.

The apps made cars easy to summon and impossible to count on. We think the trips that matter deserve better than the nearest available driver.

01 — Consistency

A standard, not a range.

A premium, commercially licensed vehicle and a career driver on every trip. The price is quoted when you book and it does not move.

02 — Reliability

A booking is a commitment.

Your driver and vehicle are assigned at booking, not auctioned at pickup. We don't cancel, and we build the business around being able to say that.

03 — Professionals

This is someone's craft.

We work only with licensed operators and career chauffeurs, and we help them grow real businesses — because the ride is only as good as the person driving it.

Who's building it

Founded by someone who still drives.

Joyride was founded by David Linhardt in Raleigh, North Carolina. He drives most weeks — partly because he likes it, mostly because it's the fastest way to find out what's actually broken about booking a car. Product decisions here come from the driver's seat and the back seat, not from a dashboard.

We're building city by city, starting close to home, and we'd rather be excellent in one market than adequate everywhere.
