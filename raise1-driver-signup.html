Product Driver onboarding

How a driver signs up to drive with Joyride.

Onboarding starts on the web at joyride.cool — one account for both riders and drivers — and continues in the Joyride Driver app, where the driver completes a background check and uploads their vehicle documents. Every screen of the real flow, in order.

1 Create & verify the account on the web  →  download the Driver app and apply with the same credentials.
Part 1

On the web — joyride.cool

Desktop & mobile browser
1

Join Joyride

The driver creates one Joyride account — first and last name, email, mobile number, and a username.

joyride.cool
2

Create the account

Set a password and create the account. Signing up accepts the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

joyride.cool
3

Verify your account

Joyride sends a 6-digit code to both email and phone. The driver verifies both to secure the account.

joyride.cool
4

Enter the codes

Phone verifies via SMS; email verifies via the emailed code. Both channels confirmed.

joyride.cool
5

Account ready — get the Driver app

With email and mobile verified, the account dashboard offers the Driver app: “apply with these same Joyride credentials.”

joyride.cool
Part 2

In the Driver app

iOS · same login
6

Background check consent

Step 1 of 3. The driver consents to a criminal background check, required by law for all drivers.

7

Personal information

Address and SSN for the check. The SSN is encrypted and used only for the background check.

8

Vehicle & documents

Step 2 of 3. Make, model, year, color, VIN, and license plate for the vehicle.

9

Upload documents

Driver's license, vehicle registration, and insurance card — photographed or scanned.

10

Documents uploaded

All three documents confirmed. The Continue button activates once everything's in.

11

Submit the application

The driver submits. Review typically takes 1–3 business days.

12

Application submitted

Step 3 of 3. Once approved — after license, insurance, vehicle, and background check — the driver can start accepting rides.
